# Four teams

[User guide](README.md) › Four teams

What each team answered when preparing its project. The daily work is the same in all four.

| | Physics, 2 people | Physics, 4 people | ML, 2 people | ML, 4 people |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Project** | Critical exponents of the diluted Ising model, for Physical Review E | Photonic crystal cavity: measurement and simulation | Open benchmark of small language models | Early prediction of sepsis in intensive care |
| **Format** | LaTeX (REVTeX) | LaTeX, with an editor other than the lead | No LaTeX, in Markdown | LaTeX |
| **Repo and disclosure** | Private; opens at milestones | Private; the patent filing with the IMPI comes before opening | Public and open from day one | Private; at milestones, with an encrypted deposit on OSF |
| **AI accounts** | Claude Pro with training turned off, with the exception recorded | Claude Team and Codex with an organization plan | Claude Pro and ChatGPT Plus; an open project needs no exception | The university's Claude Enterprise; one physician does not use AI |
| **Working from** | VS Code and the terminal | Claude Desktop, the terminal, the browser (on the lab PC) and Codex | Claude Code and Codex in the terminal | The terminal; the physician works with the commands and reviews on the GitHub website |
| **Raw data** | 40 GB of simulations on the institute's cluster | Analyzer spectra on the lab's NAS | A public dataset; the trained models, on Hugging Face | Clinical records on the hospital's server; restricted, they never go through the repo or the AI |
| **What is particular** | Each one approves the other's work | Two subparts of the same section; a task handed over during a research stay | Outside people read the repo and open Issues | A change of definition in the plan blocks a task, which is escalated to the lead |

The 2-person teams are the ones in the guides: [a day of work in physics](researcher.md#a-day-of-work-in-physics), [a day in machine learning](researcher.md#a-day-of-work-in-machine-learning) and the [setup of the physics project](lead.md#setting-up-the-project).
